Frequently Asked Questions about Power Inductors

What information should I provide for a custom power inductor?

Share the required inductance, current, operating frequency, space and mounting constraints, and any drawing or sample. Other electrical requirements can be reviewed with your application.

What power inductor constructions are available?

The listed range includes rod-type and drum-type inductors as well as custom designs, subject to review of your application and specifications.

Can power inductors be made to a customer specification?

Yes. B. R. Enterprises develops custom magnetic components according to customer drawings and agreed application requirements.
