07   COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What magnetic components does B. R. Enterprises manufacture?

B. R. Enterprises develops custom SMPS, ferrite, high-frequency, toroidal and pulse transformers; toroidal and power inductors; current transformers; EMI/RFI filters and chokes; LED-driver transformers; energy-meter coils; electronic-ballast coils; and other custom magnetic components.

Can you manufacture magnetic components to our drawing or specification?

Yes. Share your drawing, datasheet, sample or technical requirements. The team reviews the electrical and mechanical needs and discusses development against the agreed specification.

What information should I include in an enquiry?

Include the product type, application, electrical requirements you have confirmed, mechanical constraints and any drawing, circuit details, datasheet or sample. This helps the team understand your custom requirement.
